What is IQuant?

IQuant is a macroeconomic trading and market analysis platform designed to help traders understand and trade financial markets using real economic data, not speculation.


The platform focuses on top-down macro analysis, combining key global indicators such as:

  • Central bank interest rates
  • Inflation and economic growth
  • Liquidity and money supply (M2)
  • Government bond yields
  • Central bank balance sheets
  • Economic and market cycles

By integrating these indicators into a structured analytical model, IQuant provides high-quality trading signals and market insights for forex, gold, and macro-sensitive assets.


Unlike traditional trading systems that rely mainly on technical indicators, IQuant connects macroeconomics with price action, allowing traders to see the bigger picture and trade in the direction of underlying economic forces.


IQuant is built for:

  • Traders seeking institutional-style macro insights
  • Investors who want data-driven decision making
  • Users who want to understand why markets move, not just where price goes

In short, IQuant helps traders trade economic cycles, not market noise.

What data sources do you use?

IQuant uses reliable, institutional-grade data sources to ensure accuracy, consistency, and transparency in its macroeconomic analysis and trading signals.


The platform aggregates and processes data from multiple trusted providers, including:

FRED (Federal Reserve Economic Data)

Used for macroeconomic indicators such as interest rates, inflation, GDP, money supply (M2), and central bank balance sheet data.

Trading Economics

Provides global economic data, country-level indicators, forecasts, and historical time series across developed and emerging markets.

CFTC (Commitments of Traders – COT reports)

Used to analyze market positioning and sentiment of institutional traders, hedge funds, and commercial participants.

TradingView

Used for market price data, technical confirmation, and cross-market analysis across forex, commodities, and indices.

Additional public and financial data sources

Including central bank publications, official statistical agencies, and market data providers to enhance model robustness and cross-validation.


By combining these data sources, IQuant creates a unified macroeconomic framework that links economic fundamentals, market positioning, and price behavior into a single decision-making system.


This multi-source approach allows IQuant to deliver data-driven, macro-aligned trading insights that reflect real economic conditions rather than short-term market noise.
